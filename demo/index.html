Fontlex

Compilers that target fonts. Each demo is a plain text box in a compiled font: the fractions, the hidden Markdown markup and the colors are drawn by the font's rules. No script processes the text, and the characters stay exactly as typed.

Relaxed Math

Type (a+b)/(c+d) into a plain text box and the font draws a fraction. x^2 gets a superscript and sqrt(x^2+1) a root, while and/or, w/o and dates stay text. Copy a fraction and you get (a+b)/(c+d) back.

Type your own formula

Fractions, square roots, superscripts and subscripts, nested 3 deep: not LaTeX, and not a general math engine. Checked in Chrome and Safari; not yet in Firefox or on Windows.

Learner fonts

Fonts that mark how words are said or what gender a noun is. The fonts do exactly what their word lists say, in HarfBuzz and Core Text; how right the lists are is measured separately, below. They also come as a browser extension for any web page.

  • English pronunciation

    Stressed vowels, reduced vowels and silent letters of 30,000 words, from CMUdict.

    Not yet reviewed by fluent speakers; the review sheet is ready.

  • French pronunciation

    Silent letters, nasal vowels and liaison for 30,000 words, from Lexique.

    Not yet reviewed by fluent speakers; the review sheet is ready.

  • German genders

    Nouns colored by gender: der blue, die red, das green.

    On held-out newspaper text, 97% of nouns colored, 99.5% of them right.

  • German syllables

    Each word's syllables in turn blue and red, as German schools teach reading.

    97.5% of held-out first-reader words split exactly right.

Color fonts render in current Chrome, Firefox and Safari. Source, tests and the fonts: github.com/fMindstormer/fontlex · Download.
